# RACI + SLA

## Roles
- Executive Sponsor (VP)
- BI Product Owner
- Data Engineer
- Analyst (Excel owner)
- IT Support

## SLA Targets
- Daily refresh completion: before 7:00 AM local time
- Data latency tolerance: < 24h
- Critical failure alert: within 10 minutes
- Recovery objective (RTO): 2 hours

## Approval Gates
1. Data quality pass
2. KPI reconciliation vs source systems
3. Visual QA and accessibility check
